Turtle Compiler · Design system

Ink on warm paper, one colour per phase.

Light theme only. Colour always means something: a phase, a token type, or a status. Phase hues colour numbers, borders, chips and highlights. Status colours always come as a soft fill with a solid round glyph (✓ ! ✕), so they never read as a phase. The mark is the turtle's REPEAT 6 { MOVE; TURN 60; }: six sides, six phases.
